# Task-07_Decision_Making  
## Report on Decision-Making:  
A decision-oriented, auditable report for Syracuse University Women's Lacrosse may be found in this repository.  
It converts an LLM-generated story into data-driven, ethical, and proven suggestions meant to enhance player performance and lower the risk of injury.  

## Contents:  
Stakeholder_Decision_Making_Report is the final report that includes analysis, illustrations, and suggestions.  
Data & Code: Python programs, reproducibility guidelines, and artificial or anonymised datasets.  
Prompts & LLM Outputs: Human-verified modifications and original AI-generated material.  
Figures: Charts that display robustness checks, uncertainty intervals, and accuracy trends.
